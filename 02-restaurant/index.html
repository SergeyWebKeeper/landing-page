Our Story

Rooted in tradition,
plated with care

Bella Vista began as a family kitchen in Turin and grew into a table where old-world recipes meet modern technique. Every dish is made in-house daily — from stretching mozzarella to rolling tagliatelle by hand.

Our chefs source from local farms each morning, so the menu shifts gently with the seasons while the soul of the food never changes.
