Our Story

From one home oven
to your morning table

Sweet Crumb started in a tiny apartment kitchen, selling croissants to neighbors on weekends. Word spread, and eight years later we're still doing it the same way — small batches, real butter, and zero shortcuts.

Everything is baked before sunrise so it's fresh the moment we open.
