About the Studio

Photography that feels honest

Lens & Light was founded on a simple idea: the best photographs happen when people forget the camera is there. We work quietly, shoot naturally, and edit with restraint — letting real moments carry the story.

Based in the city, available worldwide for weddings, editorial work, and brand campaigns.
